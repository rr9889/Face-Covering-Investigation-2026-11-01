 STRONGS G2619: 

 κατακαλύπτω : the Sept. for כִּסָּה ; from Homer down; to cover up (see κατά , III. 3); middle present κατακαλύπτομαι , to veil or cover oneself : 1 Corinthians 11:6 ; τήν κεφαλήν , one's head, 1 Corinthians 11:7 .
