The Antiquities of the Jews, 7.254

Flavius Josephus  translated by William Whiston

« J. AJ 7.253 | J. AJ 7.254 | J. AJ 7.255 | About This Work »

254Now while the king covered himself, and grievously lamented his son, Joab went in to him, and comforted him, and said, “O my lord the king, thou art not aware that thou layest a blot on thyself by what thou now doest; for thou now seemest to hate those that love thee, and undergo dangers for thee nay, to hate thyself and thy family, and to love those that are thy bitter enemies, and to desire the company of those that are no more, and who have been justly slain;
